A session that failed to save offline gets saved before its video uploads
PR: #555 · Agent: Cloud Agent
Reported by: PostHog (clip_upload_failed, “does not reference an existing session”): 2 people on build 1.0.6 (39), 10 failed retries since Oct 18 12:52 AM PT; I confirmed neither session is in the database
What was wrong and what changed: If someone tapped Finish while offline, the save failed but the post still looked saved on the phone. Later edits and retries only tried to upload the video, to a session the server never got, so the upload failed forever. Now the app saves the session first and then the video, an edit replays the real save instead of silently changing nothing, and if the upload still can't work it stops looping and shows Retry upload. The video on the phone is never deleted. The agent checked Malcolm's hours changes (#547/#553/#554) and they did not cause this.
How to check: Turn on Airplane Mode, record a short session, tap Finish and share it (or edit its note). Turn Airplane Mode off and pull to refresh: on a friend's account the post should show up with its video, and the Uploading / Retry upload chip on your phone should clear. The two stuck posts should fix themselves the next time those people open the app.

Not duplicated (already owned by Malcolm’s bot with fix branches): zoom reset after the battery warning TRY-441, session deleted by accident TRY-421, empty video after posting a draft TRY-437.

Still waiting from earlier (4)

Stop the video-link retry loop when a clip can't stream OCT 16
PR: #523 · Agent: Cloud Agent
Reported by: PostHog “Edge function loop” alert (fired Oct 16 at 217/hr), build 39 phones on flaky cellular
What was wrong and what changed: On a flaky connection, a clip that couldn't stream kept asking our server for a video link every few seconds, for hours. Now, after a failure, the app waits before asking again (1 minute, doubling up to 10) and tries right away once the phone is back online or you scroll the post away and back. Malcolm's merged #532 already handles fully offline phones; his note says merge both.
How to check: On cellular or weak signal, leave a friend's clip that hasn't finished loading on screen. Turn airplane mode on for a minute, then off: within a few seconds the clip should play without force-quitting. Scrolling the post away and back should also retry.
Stop Latent from lingering in the Dynamic Island after you close the app OCT 17
PR: #534 · Agent: Cloud Agent
Reported by: Steven (@starz), in-app bug, build 1.0.6 (39), iOS 26.6.1, Oct 16 5:29 PM PT
What was wrong and what changed: Latent's session pill could stay in the Dynamic Island after the session ended or the app was closed, and it blocked other apps' Live Activities. Now the pill goes away as soon as a session ends (saved, discarded, recovered, or signed out), any leftover pill is cleared whenever you open Latent outside a session, and a running pill expires on its own if the app gets killed.
How to check: Start a session, then end it an awkward way (discard it, swipe Latent away in the app switcher, or sign out): the pill should disappear within a second or two. Start another session and switch to another app: the pill should stay while the session runs. Open Latent when you're not in a session: any leftover pill should clear.
Make “Edit tags…” on your profile open the tag editor OCT 17
PR: #535 · Agent: Cloud Agent
Reported by: migue (@m1guelch), in-app bug, build 1.0.6 (39), iOS 26.5.2, Oct 17 1:50 AM PT
What was wrong and what changed: On your profile, “Edit tags…” in the tag filter menu looked tappable, but taps fell through to the chart underneath. Even when a tap landed, iOS 26 dropped the editor because it opened at the same moment the menu closed. Now the tap reaches the button and the editor opens right after the menu closes. Nothing new was built; it just reaches the tag editor that already exists.
How to check: Open You, tap the sliders chip at the end of the tag row, then tap “Edit tags…”: the tag editor should slide up. Add, rename, or delete a tag and tap Done; the profile chips should update. Holding a tag chip and tapping Edit tag should open the same editor.
Don't send people to Settings when there's no Camera switch OCT 17
PR: #536 · Agent: Cloud Agent
Reported by: “Camera Access” email to support@, Oct 16 9:28 AM PT (sender address hidden)
What was wrong and what changed: iPhone only shows a Camera switch under Settings › Latent after the app has asked for the camera once, and hides it when Screen Time blocks the camera. Latent treated both cases like “they said no” and sent people to a Settings page with nothing to turn on. Now it shows the normal Allow prompt if it hasn't asked yet, says plainly when Screen Time is blocking the camera, and only opens Settings after someone tapped Don't Allow.
How to check: On a real iPhone, delete and reinstall Latent and open Record: you should see “Turn on camera” and the iOS Allow prompt, not Open Settings. Tap Don't Allow, then Open Settings: Settings › Latent should now list Camera. With Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions › Camera set to Don't Allow, Record should show a short message and no Settings button.
