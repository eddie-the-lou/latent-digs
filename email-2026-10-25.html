#617 Record: Undo after Finish no longer uploads an empty clip waiting for review

What was wrong and what changes: On the new 1.0.9, if someone taps Finish, then Post, and then Undo to keep recording, the post they started was already saved and uploading. Undo threw away its video file, so the upload found an empty file (8 people overnight), the first post ended up as Video missing, and finishing again made a second copy of the session. Now Undo cancels that pending post and its upload, keeps all the footage, and finishing again saves exactly one session with the full video. The agent added tests but couldn't run them (no Xcode on its machine), so run them or try it on a phone.

How to check: Record a short private session, tap Finish, go through to Post, and right away tap Undo to keep recording. Record a bit more and finish again. You should get one post with the whole video, no Video missing post and no duplicate.

Found by: Found by me in PostHog (8 people on 1.0.9 (47) since about 12:30 AM PT today; nobody has written in about it yet). Agent: https://cursor.com/agents/bc-b3087cd1-9a3d-5da8-b01d-cce9f75051ed
