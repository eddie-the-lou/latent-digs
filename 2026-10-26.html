#633 Stop retrying empty-clip uploads on every launch waiting for review

What was wrong and what changes: On 1.0.9, a few phones had a post whose video file was empty or gone, and the app tried to upload it again on nearly every open, forever (48 tries in about 18 hours for one person). These people never tapped Undo. Two causes, found by reading the code: the 'give up after 3 tries' count reset every time the app opened, and an empty file was treated as a real video (so leftover recording pieces that could rebuild it could get cleaned up). Now an empty or missing video waits one launch in case it's still finishing, then tries to rebuild from leftover pieces, and otherwise stops retrying and shows the existing Video missing label. The post is kept, good videos are never deleted, and no duplicate is made. This PR is built on top of #617, so it includes that fix too: merge #617 first or merge both. Tests were added but couldn't run (no Xcode on the agent's machine).

How to check: Run the new tests in Xcode (EmptyClipRetryTests and #617's FinishUndoTests; the PR lists the command). On a phone with a Debug build: do Finish, Post, then Undo right away, and you should get one post with its video and no Video missing post. Record and save a normal session and make sure friends see the video. After it ships, PostHog should show one clip_upload_abandoned per stuck post instead of an empty-clip failure on every app open.

Found by: Found by me in PostHog (7 people on 1.0.9 (47) with an empty video retried on every app open, no Undo involved; nobody has written in about it). Agent: https://cursor.com/agents/bc-9e2fdb68-ca7e-50e6-bf4b-5d4f05cb964a
