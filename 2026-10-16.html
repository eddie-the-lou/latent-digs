Stop the clip stream fallback retry loop on bad networks
PR: #523 · Agent: Cloud Agent
What it fixes: On bad cellular, the app kept re-asking our server for a video link in a loop for hours. One phone hit ~794 failed retries in a single evening (Oct 15, 5:05–10:54 PM PT), a second ~370 this morning, and PostHog's “Edge function loop” alert fired at 217 an hour. The fix makes the app back off after a failure instead of retrying right away, and recover on its own when the network comes back.
How to check: On cellular or weak signal, leave a friend's clip that hasn't finished loading on screen. Turn airplane mode on for a minute, then off: within a few seconds the clip should play without force-quitting. Scrolling the post away and back should also retry.
Found from: PostHog · “Edge function loop” alert firing (217/hr) · ~1,265 failed retries since yesterday 9 AM PT · build 39, mostly 2 phones on cellular.
