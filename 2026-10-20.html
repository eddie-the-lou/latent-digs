Saves retried when you reopen the app no longer go out as if you're signed out NEW · OCT 20
PR: #568 · Agent: Cloud Agent · Status: PR open (draft), not merged
Reported by: PostHog (session_save_failed, “permission denied for table work_types”, sent as the signed-out role): 6 people in 7 days on builds 1.0.6 (39) and 1.0.7 (41), 4 of them since yesterday's review. Each one fired within a second of the app reopening after 45+ minutes in the background. I couldn't find 4 of those 6 sessions in the database afterwards, and one person's video then got stuck uploading to a post that never saved.
What was wrong and what changed: When someone saved a session and then left the app for about an hour, the save that retries as the app reopens could go out before the app had signed the person back in. The server treated it like a logged-out visitor and refused it (“permission denied for table work_types”), so the post never reached the server and its video then got stuck. Now the app waits until the person is signed in again before sending, retries once if it is still refused, and never throws away the session or its video. A normal save without leaving the app works the same as today.
How to check: On a phone with this build: sign in, record a short session, save it (with a clip, shared with friends), then put Latent in the background right away and leave it for more than an hour. Reopen Latent: the post should still be on your profile, a friend should see it with a playable video, and PostHog should show no “permission denied for table work_types” save failure for that reopen. The agent couldn't compile it (no Mac), so it asks you to run the Mac tests and this phone check before merging.

Not duplicated: nico's lost session, the stuck uploads and the empty clips are already fixed on main (#555, #557); the new freeze and crash tickets belong to Malcolm's bot (TRY-468 with #567, TRY-469, TRY-470, TRY-471).
