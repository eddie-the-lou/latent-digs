Fixes I opened today (1, waiting on review)

  • #653 Keep Spotify playing when you start a session
    Reported by: ❤️ 201k [OILY] in Discord #general-chat (Oct 28, 8:25 AM PT), and charan agreed (8:43 AM PT)
    What was wrong: Starting a session stops whatever the person was listening to on Spotify or Apple Music. The app is meant to let their music keep playing.
    What changed: iOS lets the camera grab the phone's sound by default, even though Latent never records sound, and that paused Spotify and Apple Music when the camera started. The fix tells the camera not to touch the phone's sound (plus a test). Camera-off, the timer alarm and full-screen video were already fine and aren't touched.
    How to check: On a real phone (the simulator can't test this): play Spotify, open Latent, tap Record with the camera on. The song should keep going through pause, resume and Finish. Also try camera off. The timer alarm may briefly lower the song, full-screen videos still take over the sound, and feed songs still autoplay, all as before.
    Agent: https://cursor.com/agents/bc-6c8e9fe4-fda3-5d17-97eb-0336191e0014

Everything else that's clearly broken already has a ticket from Malcolm's bot: profile picture loads forever (TRY-598), apps stay blocked (TRY-602), the deleted post (TRY-603). JiaYi's black camera looks like a declined camera prompt she later allowed.
