Latent against typical and exceptional apps

Retention counts opening the app on that day or in that week, like the published benchmarks. "Exceptional" is a16z's OK / good / great bands for consumer social apps. Latent is a three-days-a-week product, so a perfect week of the streak scores about 43% on the daily ÷ monthly line, and the daily bars are not the ones to chase.
