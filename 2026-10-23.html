A camera-off session no longer counts the whole night after an App Store update closes Latent NEW
PR: #601 · Agent: Cloud Agent · Status: Waiting for your review (draft PR, merges cleanly, not merged)
Reported by: Danny (@danny) in Discord #general-chat, Oct 22, 3:27 PM and 7:17 PM PT (“i recorded a latent session as i went to sleep … when i woke up latent wasn’t on, and i was using my phone like normal … so i stopped and it had been 11 hours”). You already asked him “what happened?” at 6:39 PM. PostHog: his camera-off session on 1.0.7 started 12:41 AM PT, the App Store updated Latent to 1.0.8 at 3:10 AM PT (which closes the app), and when he opened it at 11:44 AM PT all 11 hours 3 minutes were counted with 2 seconds paused. 8 recovered sessions from 6 people got 3+ extra hours this way in the last week (some may be real overnight sessions).
What was wrong and what changed: When you record with the camera off and lock the phone, Latent keeps counting, and if iPhone closes Latent in the meantime it credits the time up to when you next open it (capped at 12 hours). An automatic App Store update closed Danny's Latent at 3:10 AM and removed the Lock Screen timer, so he woke up thinking nothing was recording and used his phone for hours, but Latent still counted everything until he opened it at 11:44 AM. Latent only settled the locked time when someone opened the app, and the quiet restart after the update never did. Now, when Latent starts again on a new build (an update) or finds the Lock Screen timer already gone, the counted time stops at that moment and the session comes back paused so the person can resume or finish. The normal case (lock in Latent, work, unlock back into Latent) still counts the full time. PostHog also gets a new field saying why the time stopped.
How to check: On a phone: start a camera-off (Photo + timer) session and lock the phone in Latent. While it's locked, install a new build over it (TestFlight or Xcode), wait a while and use other apps, then open Latent. The time should stop at the install, not when you opened it, and the session should be paused, not lost. Then check the normal path: lock in Latent, wait about 10 minutes, unlock back into Latent: about 10 minutes should count. The agent couldn't compile (no Mac), so run LockedRunRecoveryTests and LiveSessionRestoreTests on a Mac first.
